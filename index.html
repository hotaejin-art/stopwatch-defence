Where are my TICKS and skins?

Your TICKS balance and unlocked skins are stored on your account. Just sign in with the same account (Apple, Google, or email) and they will be there. There is no separate "restore" step for TICKS.

Purchases and refunds

Payments are processed by Apple. For billing questions or refund requests, use Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com.

Device-local progress

Challenge badges and some unlock progress are stored on your device and are not synced between devices.

Delete my account

In the app: open the TICKS wallet, sign in, and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, TICKS balance, unlocked skins, and synced progress, including your Global Records entries. Or email hotaejin@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.

Global Records

Sign in before starting a run, then enter a nickname when you set a new record to post it to the in-game Global Records. To report an offensive nickname, email hotaejin@gmail.com.
