Stopwatch Defence

Privacy Policy · Effective September 28, 2026

Stopwatch Defence ("the Game") is developed by Taejin Nam ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Game collects and how it is used. It applies to the iOS app and its online features.

Data We Collect

  • Email address — collected when you create an account or sign in with Apple, Google, or email. Used only for authentication.
  • User ID — a random account identifier assigned to your account. Used to identify your wallet and entitlements.
  • Purchase history — records of TICKS pack purchases and redemptions (transaction IDs and amounts) so your balance is credited exactly once and refunds can be processed.
  • Product interaction — gameplay progress you submit for TICKS rewards (difficulty level, boss defeats, stage clears).

How We Use Data

All collected data is used only to operate the Game's core functionality: signing you in, keeping your TICKS balance and unlocked skins on your account, and processing purchases. We do not use your data for advertising, tracking, analytics, profiling, or sale.

Linked to You

The data above is linked to your account. We do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, health data, or diagnostics, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Storage and Service Providers

Account data is stored on our backend (Supabase). Sign-in is processed by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies when you use those options. Preferences such as selected skin and music settings are stored only on your device. Challenge badges and some progress are also device-local.

Retention and Deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time directly in the app: open the TICKS wallet, sign in, and choose Delete Account. Deletion permanently removes your account, TICKS balance, unlocked skins, and synced achievement progress. Anonymous purchase records that no longer identify your account may be retained for accounting purposes. You can also request deletion by emailing hotaejin@gmail.com.

Children

The Game is rated 13+ and is not directed to children under 13.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting hotaejin@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
